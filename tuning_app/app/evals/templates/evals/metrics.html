Metrics

Every eval run records the metric version it was scored with. An output that isn't a valid page scores 0 on everything.

Faithfulness
Half deterministic (the share of generated operations, parameters and types found in the gold page or the parser's output), half judge (the share of the judge's claims that the source supports).
Component accuracy
Field-level agreement with the gold page: operation F1 by derived ID, then parameter names, locations, types, required flags and defaults, returns, signatures and groups of matched operations, as a weighted mean.
Coverage
The share of gold operations present.
Example validity
The share of checkable examples that hold up: JSON parses, Python parses, curl paths exist in the gold surface. No checkable examples scores 0.
Prose quality
The judge's 1–5 rating, scaled to 0–1.
